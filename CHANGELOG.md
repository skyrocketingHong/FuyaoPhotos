# Changelog

## 27.0 — 2026-09-21

Initial release of Fuyao Photo Info for Android 8.0 and later.

### Photo cards

- Add a rounded, frosted information card with editable device, photographer, location, lens and capture details.
- Read available EXIF data and resolve photo GPS locations, with manual editing and saved photographer/lens profiles.
- Customize card size, text size, spacing, blur, opacity and corners; import custom fonts.
- Edit up to 50 photos in one session with independent information and styles.

### Preview and export

- Compare with the original, zoom into full-resolution photos, play embedded Motion Photo video and switch HDR preview.
- Export original-resolution JPEG/PNG with adjustable JPEG quality and global save defaults that can be overridden per save.
- Independently retain or remove capture details, location and capture time, including supported motion-video metadata.
- Preserve supported JPEG Ultra HDR gainmaps and Motion Photo audio/video while editing the cover, without video transcoding.
- Open photos from the system picker, Files or Android share menu; open or share completed exports.

### Interface and compatibility

- Material 3 controls, dynamic themes, compact media tools, adaptive layouts, English and Simplified Chinese.
- SF Symbols SVG media icons and Google Material Symbols for standard actions.
- Saved settings and editing state, unsaved-change confirmation and actionable error messages.
- HDR display requires Android 14+ and compatible hardware. Unrecognized media containers or unverifiable metadata removal stop export.
